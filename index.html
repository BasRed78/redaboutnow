The short version

Half British, half Dutch. I grew up between two countries that approach problems very differently. One apologises before giving feedback, the other gives it without waiting for permission. I've learned to do both.

My career looks like someone couldn't make up their mind, but there's a thread: I like new or complex situations where the brand, the marketing or the team needs work.

Each time the job was the same: untangle the complexity and help teams move with focus. That might mean defining positioning, leading through change or building a marketing function from scratch, usually when things are moving fast, not quite clear, or too important to get wrong.

I build things now too. Partly because I can, partly because I've seen gaps that annoyed me enough to do something about them. Fortitude and Seen both have roots in my own experience. Billy came from watching my father struggle with the consequences of what happened to him.

Based in Amsterdam. London and LA feel like second homes. Happiest when I'm slightly out of my depth, working with people who care more about getting it right than getting credit.
